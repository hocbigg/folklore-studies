# Great Courses in Folklore Studies

This curated collection features full-length university OpenCourseWare, massive open online courses (MOOCs), and authoritative public lecture series in Folklore Studies. These freely accessible audiovisual resources explore oral epic traditions, vernacular religion, sociolinguistic performance, indigenous knowledge systems, and public heritage safeguarding.

## Oral Epic, Myth, and Traditional Narrative

- [The Ancient Greek Hero in 24 Hours (Harvard University / edX / Gregory Nagy)](https://www.edx.org/learn/humanities/harvard-university-the-ancient-greek-hero)
- [Finding Britain's Lost Gods (Gresham College / Ronald Hutton)](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/watch-now/series/lost-gods)

## Vernacular Belief, Ritual, and Magic

- [Magic in the Middle Ages (University of Barcelona / Coursera / Pau Castell and Delfi Nieto-Isabel)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/magic-middle-ages)
- [Modern Paganism and Witchcraft (Gresham College / Ronald Hutton)](https://www.gresham.ac.uk/watch-now/series/modern-paganism-and-witchcraft)

## Indigenous Traditions and Oral Epistemologies

- [Indigenous Canada (University of Alberta / Coursera / Tracy Bear and Paul Gareau)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/indigenous-canada)
- [Aboriginal Worldviews and Education (University of Toronto / Coursera / Jean-Paul Restoule)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/aboriginal-education)

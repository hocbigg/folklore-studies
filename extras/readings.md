# Great Readings in Folklore Studies

This curated reading list features the discipline-defining monographs, landmark theoretical papers, and seminal ethnographic studies that have shaped modern folkloristics. These works illustrate how folklorists analyze informal expressive culture, vernacular belief systems, artistic performance, and community traditions.

# Advanced Topics in Folklore Studies

After completing the core undergraduate curriculum, learners can branch into advanced specializations. These tracks prepare students for graduate research, archival management, public sector cultural advocacy, and ethnographic documentation.

### Specialization Tracks

- **Public Folklore, Cultural Policy, and Applied Folkloristics:** Focuses on cultural conservation, museum ethnography, festival curation, and international heritage governance.
- **Vernacular Religion, Belief Systems, and Contemporary Legend:** Focuses on lived religion, supernatural experience narratives, contemporary legend dynamics, and collective anxieties.
- **Digital Folkloristics, Algorithmic Vernaculars, and Media Ecology:** Focuses on internet-native folklore, memetics, participatory culture, and the folkloric dynamics of platform algorithms.
- **Indigenous Epistemologies, Decoloniality, and Diasporic Traditions:** Focuses on indigenous narrative sovereignty, landscape ethnography, decolonial research ethics, and diasporic cultural retention.
- **Ethnopoetics, Sociolinguistics, and Expressive Performance:** Focuses on the structural transcription of oral verbal art, sociolinguistic registers, and communicative competence.
